About M Studio

Interior design studio in Phuket, Thailand

M Studio is an interior design studio based in Phuket, Thailand, specializing in complete interior solutions for private villas, condominiums and investment properties.

Founded by interior designer Maryana Kotova, M Studio creates warm, contemporary interiors designed around the way each property will actually be used — whether as a private home, holiday residence or rental investment.

Our work goes beyond interior design. We provide a complete turnkey furnishing service in Phuket, including space planning, furniture selection and custom furniture, lighting, appliances, outdoor furniture, artwork, textiles, kitchenware, bedding, décor, delivery, installation and final styling.

We work closely with property owners, developers, contractors and furniture manufacturers throughout the project, coordinating the details from the initial concept to the final setup of the property.

M Studio has experience working with villas and residences across Phuket, including Bang Tao, Layan and Cherngtalay, as well as projects in developments such as Botanica and Clover Residence.

Our approach combines functionality, comfort and a relaxed contemporary aesthetic suited to tropical living. We favor natural materials, warm tones and timeless solutions rather than interiors created only for visual impact.

For investment properties, we also provide home staging and furnishing solutions designed to make the property attractive, practical and ready for rental or sale.

Interior Design Services in Phuket

M Studio Interior Design covers the whole interior of a property in Phuket, from the first concept to the final setup.

01

Interior Design in Phuket

Interior design for private homes, holiday residences and rental properties across Phuket. Each interior is planned around the way the property will actually be used, with natural materials and warm tones suited to tropical living.

02

Villa Interior Design

Complete interiors for private villas in Bang Tao, Layan, Cherngtalay and other areas of Phuket. We work with owners, developers and contractors from the initial concept to the final setup of the villa.

03

Condominium Interior Design

Interiors for condominiums and apartments — for owners who live in them and for investors who rent them out.

04

Turnkey Furnishing

A complete turnkey furnishing service in Phuket — furniture, lighting, appliances, outdoor furniture, artwork, textiles, kitchenware, bedding and décor, with delivery, installation and final styling.

05

Furniture Packages

Furniture packages for villas and condominiums, selected for the layout and for how the property will be used — as a private home, holiday residence or rental.

06

Custom Furniture

Custom indoor and outdoor furniture made to order from natural materials at factories in Chiang Mai, Bangkok and Indonesia.

07

Home Staging

Home staging and furnishing for investment properties, so they are attractive, practical and ready for rental or sale.

08

Interior Styling

Interior styling and decoration — artwork, textiles, décor, outdoor styling and the final setup of the property.

Interior Design & Renovation

Our Interior Design & Renovation service is for clients who want to transform a property completely — from the layout to the final finishing details.

M Studio develops the full interior concept and manages the changes needed to bring it to life. Depending on the project, this can include space planning and layout changes, selection of materials and finishes, walls, flooring, windows and doors, lighting design and electrical work, bathroom renovation, and built-in furniture such as wardrobes, kitchens and storage.

We coordinate the project from start to finish, so the client does not have to manage contractors, suppliers and installation schedules separately. Every decision — from materials to lighting — is made with the architecture of the property and the way the space will be used in mind.

Once the renovation is complete, we can also furnish and style the property, so it is ready to live in or to rent out.

Interior Design & Renovation is suitable for villas and condominiums that need a full update, a new layout or a completely new interior style.

From the first plan to the final detail, M Studio creates interiors that are ready for living.

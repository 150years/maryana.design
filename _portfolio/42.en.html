This project at Manor Phuket was completed by M Studio as a full furniture package developed specifically for the villa.

The architecture of the house already included a number of soft, rounded elements, and our goal was to continue this design language throughout the furniture and interior styling. We repeated these curved forms in selected furniture pieces and details so that the interior would feel naturally connected to the architecture rather than visually separate from it.

For this project, we used solid teak wood furniture, chosen for its durability, natural texture and suitability for the tropical climate of Phuket. The furniture was custom-made specifically for the villa according to the dimensions and proportions of each space.

The pieces were produced at our factory in Indonesia, where we were able to control the materials, proportions and overall quality of the furniture throughout the production process. This allowed us to create a cohesive collection that works as one complete concept rather than a mix of unrelated individual items.

The final result is a warm, contemporary interior with a natural tropical character. The combination of solid teak, soft curves and carefully selected proportions creates a calm and balanced atmosphere that complements the architectural style of Manor Phuket.

Every piece was developed as part of M Studio’s complete furniture package, with attention to both functionality and the overall visual harmony of the villa.

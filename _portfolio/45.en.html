This project at Anchan Indigo was completed by M Studio as an interior design project developed in accordance with the client’s brief and lifestyle requirements. Unlike a full furniture package, our role here was focused on creating the design concept, planning the spaces and developing tailored interior solutions for the villa.

The design was created with careful attention to the client’s specific needs and the way the family would use each area of the home. In this project, we designed several important functional spaces, including a sauna, a children’s bedroom with a dedicated play area, and a private home office for the owner. Each room was planned to be both practical and visually cohesive, while maintaining a calm, elegant atmosphere throughout the villa.

Special attention was also given to the outdoor living area. One of the key design features is the natural stone outdoor fireplace, which adds warmth, texture and character to the space. In the evening, it creates a beautiful and inviting atmosphere, making the outdoor area feel especially cosy and comfortable for relaxing or spending time with family and guests.

The result is a refined and thoughtfully designed villa interior that reflects the client’s vision while combining comfort, function and timeless style.

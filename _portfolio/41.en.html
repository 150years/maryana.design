This project was completed as a full furniture package by M Studio, with the furniture, décor and styling developed specifically for this villa.

The interior was designed in a contemporary Asian style, combining natural materials, handcrafted furniture and a light, calm color palette. Following the client’s preference for bright and airy interiors, we kept the overall atmosphere soft and neutral, allowing the natural textures of wood to become an important part of the design.

Many furniture pieces were custom-made from solid teak wood according to the exact dimensions and proportions of each room. Teak was chosen for its natural beauty, durability and timeless character, making it especially suitable for tropical living.

To add depth and individuality to the contemporary interior, we incorporated selected antique pieces made from carved wood and sourced from India. These unique elements bring a sense of history and craftsmanship to the space while creating a beautiful contrast with the clean, modern lines of the new furniture.

Several tables were also custom-made from solid mango wood, adding another warm and natural texture to the interior.

The result is a light, comfortable and timeless villa interior where contemporary Asian design, traditional craftsmanship and natural materials come together in a relaxed and elegant way. Every furniture piece was selected or created specifically for the property as part of M Studio’s complete furniture package.
